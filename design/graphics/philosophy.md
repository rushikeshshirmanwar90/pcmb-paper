# Desk Brutalism

Objects as cut card on a warm desk. Every form is a flat plane of confident colour held by one unbroken ink line, lifted a few millimetres off the page by a hard shadow that falls down and to the right, always the same distance, always the same direction. Nothing is rendered or shaded; everything is placed. The work should feel meticulously crafted, each piece cut, aligned and pinned by someone who has spent years at the bench.

Space is generous and calm. One subject sits at the centre with clear air around it, so it reads like a sticker on a notebook cover: recognisable as a black silhouette at the size of a thumbnail, satisfying at full size. Secondary marks (a sparkle, a dot, a short dash) orbit the subject in small numbers and never compete with it. Every gap is deliberate, and the product of painstaking attention to balance.

Colour is rationed. Warm yellow is the loud voice; pink, mint and lavender are the quiet ones, and no piece uses more than one loud and two quiet colours. Ink and white paper carry the rest. There are no gradients, glows, textures or soft edges; flatness is the material, and its honesty is the point.

Line is the signature. A single weight runs through every outline, with rounded joins and rounded ends, so a set of pieces looks like one hand drew them in one sitting. Inside a shape, details are drawn with the same ink but kept clear of the outer outline, so the silhouette stays clean. Master-level execution shows in what is left out.

Rhythm comes from repetition across the set, not decoration within a piece: the same viewpoint, the same shadow, the same proportions of colour to ink. Seen together, the pieces should feel like a family of tools on one desk, the result of countless refinements rather than a single lucky drawing.

## App Art Contract (wins on any conflict; from neobrutalism-expo/references/graphics.md §2)
1. Palette: only the student look's hexes: ink #121212, white #FFFFFF, yellow #F7C744 / #FCEDBE, pink #F49AD9, mint #86DE98, lavender #BFA3FF, sky #8FD3FF. No gradients, textures or shading.
2. Line: a uniform ink outline of 2dp at the target size, rounded joins.
3. Depth: flat fills plus the hard offset shadow (down-right, ink, zero blur).
4. Composition: one clear subject, centred, ≥ 6% safe padding, transparent background; reads as a silhouette at 64dp.
5. No text, numbers or letters inside images.
6. Interior ink never touches the outer outline (clean dark variants).
7. No people.
8. Original only: nothing traced or recreated from any reference image, artist or brand.
9. A set, not singles: same viewpoint, line weight, palette ratios and shadow direction.
10. Masters rendered at ≥ 3× the target size.
