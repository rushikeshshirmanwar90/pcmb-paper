# Who we design for: 11th–12th PCMB students (16–20, Gen Z, India)

Every engagement decision starts here. The goal is **more effective study time and better marks**. Usage is how we get there, never the goal itself.

## The student

- **Age 16–20**, in 11th or 12th (or a drop year), preparing for **JEE (Main/Advanced), NEET, MHT-CET or the HSC/board exams**, usually at a coaching tuition plus school.
- **Many are minors.** Design as if a parent and a teacher will read every screen and notification.
- **Phone-native, short-form native.** Their attention competes with Reels, Shorts and games. Sessions are short and frequent: a bus ride, between classes, before dinner.
- **Time-poor:** school + coaching + homework + tests. Typical free practice windows are 10–30 minutes, often in the evening.
- **High stakes, high pressure:** rank-driven exams, family expectations, constant comparison ("he got 98 percentile"), fear of falling behind.

## What motivates them (use these)

| Motivator | What it means in the app |
|---|---|
| **Visible progress** | Mastery bars that grow, chapters turning "mastered", expected marks rising. Progress they can *see* beats points they can't use |
| **Competence and quick wins** | Start every session with an achievable question; daily goals that fit 10 minutes; "you fixed 3 old mistakes" |
| **Exam relevance** | Show *why it matters*: "Asked in NEET 2022", "This chapter carries ~12 marks", "Your weakest high-weight chapter" |
| **Autonomy** | They choose the daily goal, which chapter, duel or not, hide from the league. No forced paths or locked content |
| **Recognition from people they respect** | A teacher's shout-out is worth more than any badge. Batch-level recognition is meaningful but must stay kind |
| **Belonging** | Study squads and batch challenges: "we did it together", not "I beat you" |
| **Identity and self-expression** | Levels with fun science titles, profile frames, a streak they're proud of, a weekly recap worth sharing |
| **Novelty in small doses** | A new daily challenge, a weekly league, occasional new badges. Fresh, never chaotic |

## What hurts them (design against these)

| Risk | Design response |
|---|---|
| **Comparison anxiety** | Leaderboards rank **effort (XP)** within the batch, reset weekly, show top 10 + you only, never marks, and anyone can hide. The default comparison is **you vs. last week's you** |
| **Fear of failure / fixed mindset** | Mistakes are framed as "fuel": the Mistakes notebook, "+15 XP for fixing it". Low scores get calm next steps, never red screens or shame |
| **Burnout and sleep loss** | No nudges 10 PM–7 AM; after 11 PM a gentle "sleep helps memory, see you tomorrow" card instead of more challenges; daily XP caps; streaks forgive (freezes, test-day protection) |
| **Procrastination via "fake productivity"** | XP comes from *learning actions* (answering, fixing mistakes, finishing tests), not app opens, scrolling or speed-tapping |
| **Test anxiety** | Nothing gamified inside the secure test runner: no XP pops, timers that shout, or streak warnings. Rewards appear after results are released |
| **Exhaustion from notifications** | Gentle nudges: at most 1 per day, at the student's chosen time, only if today's goal isn't done |
| **Social risk** | No free-text chat between students; reactions and preset messages only. Duels and squads only within the student's own batch |

## Engagement rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Learning-first metrics:** a feature ships only if it plausibly increases correct practice, revision of mistakes, or test participation. Time-in-app alone is not success.
2. **No gambling mechanics:** no loot boxes, spin wheels, mystery rewards, chance-based drops or paid currency. Rewards are earned, predictable and shown upfront.
3. **No shame or guilt:** no "you're letting your streak die", crying mascots, red-flagged ranks, public bottom ranks, or "your friends are ahead of you".
4. **No fake urgency or scarcity:** countdowns only for real deadlines (tests, exams, weekly league end).
5. **Forgiving streaks:** freezes, repair, exam-day protection, and a streak shown as a celebration, not a debt.
6. **Privacy:** batchmates see name, level, weekly XP and badges only. **Marks and accuracy are never shown to other students.** Teachers see engagement plus the learning data they already have.
7. **Respect rest:** quiet hours, a late-night wind-down card, daily caps, and no rewards for studying past midnight.
8. **Opt-outs everywhere:** hide from the league, decline duels, turn off nudges, reduce motion, turn off vibration.

## Voice and copy (Gen Z, Indian, exam-prep)

- **Short, warm, a little witty, never cringe.** Talk like a smart senior who has been through JEE/NEET, not a brand mascot or a meme page.
- At most one emoji per message. No forced slang ("no cap", "slay"). Light, real humor only.
- English by default, with exam vocabulary they use: PYQ, chapter, mock, percentile, revision, backlog.
- Be specific: "3 questions to finish today's goal" beats "Keep going!".
- Celebrate effort and improvement: "Accuracy in Rotational Motion: 38% → 61% this week."

| Moment | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Daily reminder | "Today's 5 are ready, about 6 min. Kinematics first 🎯" | "Don't lose your streak!!! 😱" |
| Streak at risk | "2 questions left to keep your 12-day streak." | "Your streak is dying" |
| Low test score | "Tough one. 4 topics to fix, starting with Electrostatics (worth ~8 marks)." | "You failed" / a red screen |
| Mistake fixed | "Fixed! That one won't get you again. +15 XP" | "Finally correct." |
| Level up | "Level 10: Compound ⚗️ You've solved 1,200 questions." | "OMG LEVEL UP!!!" |
| Late night | "It's past 11. Sleep locks in today's learning. Your streak is safe 🌙" | "One more challenge?" |
| League | "You're #4 this week. 80 XP to #3." | "You're losing to Riya" |

## Personas for design reviews

- **Aarav (JEE, 12th, ambitious):** wants rank, PYQs and speed. Loves the league and duels; needs guardrails against burnout.
- **Sneha (NEET, 11th, anxious):** worried about Physics. Needs gentle wins, the Mistakes notebook and private progress; probably hides from the league.
- **Rohit (MHT-CET/boards, inconsistent):** studies in bursts. Needs streak freezes, a 10-minute daily challenge and a teacher's shout-out to come back.

Walk every new screen through all three. If it helps Aarav but scares Sneha or loses Rohit, change it.
